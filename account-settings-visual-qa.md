# Account Settings Visual QA

The protected `?view=account` surface was verified at 1440x1100 and 390x1200. Desktop renders the profile preferences, passwordless recovery, JSON export, and destructive deletion sections in a balanced two-column layout. Mobile stacks the same sections vertically, keeps inputs and controls within the viewport, and preserves readable touch targets. The destructive action remains visibly separated and disabled until the exact confirmation phrase is entered.
